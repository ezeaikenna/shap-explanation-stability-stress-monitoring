The RELAX participant-level pseudonymised exports remain participant- and observation-linked: row indices, timestamps, and combinations of attributes may permit linkage. Pseudonymisation is not anonymisation. Treat all generated row-level and participant-linked files as restricted research data unless an appropriate privacy and disclosure review explicitly authorises release.

The SSAQS prediction-comparison and held-out exports contain participant/time-linked predictions or SHAP information. The RELAX global held-out export contains participant-linked predictions and SHAP values.

Notebook cell outputs were cleared while executable source cells were retained. The notebooks can generate excluded row-level outputs when run with the required source inputs. Do not commit generated restricted outputs to this repository.
